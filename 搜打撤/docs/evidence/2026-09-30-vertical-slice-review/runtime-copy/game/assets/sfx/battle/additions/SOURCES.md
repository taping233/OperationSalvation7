Suggested runtime paths are `assets/sfx/battle/additions/<file>`. The sounds are mono/stereo Vorbis OGG at 44.1 kHz; measured durations are 0.043 s, 0.110 s, 0.954 s, and 0.777 s respectively. Their sizes total about 65 KB. `danger` has no included asset: the selected packs do not offer a clearly identified, short warning cue suitable for this game's combat. VO is also not included; this game needs role-specific Chinese lines and casting/recording rather than generic English prompts.

Files were copied without resampling or gain processing. Listening validation and peak/loudness normalization remain for in-game mix integration.
