LIMITS
- Generator rejects candidate graphs internally before returning: 100 quality passes are post-filter outputs, not first-attempt acceptance rates.
- Graph reachability ignores player HP, card cost, lock conditions, event outcomes and ability to defeat BOSS; it does not prove gameplay completion or evacuation usability.
- Node types are map containers: battle count excludes battles started by events or other node contents.
- Seed set matches batch-0..99 in existing generator test and is reproducible, not a random population sample.
