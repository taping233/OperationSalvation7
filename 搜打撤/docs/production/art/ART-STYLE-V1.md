# 游戏默认画风 v1.0

2026-10-15 老板明确恢复游戏默认画风为 `artifact-template-anime-block-brush` 的历史打包版 v1.0。本页是项目默认画法的现行正文；人物身份、用途、构图和光色按任务分别指定。技能后续版本与 NAI 配方版本不自动覆盖本页。既有运行素材不因本次定调自动替换。

## 画法

- 手绘二次元块面厚涂：不透明、概括的综合色块与少数宽弧形或长条覆盖笔塑形，主要明暗边界明确，局部肤色可柔和过渡。笔触在转折与覆盖处出现，大片内部保持安静，不铺满纹理或碎三角面。
- 头发由少量圆转、相扣的宽发束构成。每束以底色、一道宽阴影和少数不规则短弧亮笔表现体积，避免密集尖束、细发网和碎高光。
- 二次元脸眼：清楚上眼睑、简洁虹膜色块及两三处高光；鼻口经济，暖肤色大面完整。脸比衣料细化，保留刘海、太阳穴、鼻底与颈部必要投影，不画成半写实精修脸。
- 衣料归成三四组主要褶皱，以宽笔及相连主投影说明体积；白衣保留两三块可读阴影，不拆成密集白色尖片。关键手势和道具结构清楚。
- 灰化综合色铺大面，冷暖有区别，局部鲜色点睛；不全图统一褐灰，也不要求所有题材使用同一配色。环境采用少数清楚的大形和稀疏笔触，光源由场景决定。
- 轻量结构增量：眼下、下颌至颈部、领口与肩胸主褶可有少数收尖彩线和中间调；保留活的覆盖笔，不把所有表面压成平填。不用全局颗粒、磨皮、摄影虚化或塑料高光代替画法。

## 可复用画风段

以下英文段用于与原 v1.0 画法对齐；主体、配色、构图和背景内容另写。

```text
STYLE REFERENCES: original images 5, 8, 1 in that order. Image 5 anchors brush scale, chunky locks and cloth; image 8 anchors quiet broad clothing and economical facial drawing; image 1 anchors clearly anime eyes and minimal nose/mouth. Borrow painting grammar only, not characters, costumes, accessories or poses.
HAIR: a few large rounded interlocking locks, broad coherent curved silhouettes and quiet unbroken interior color areas. Simple local base, one broad cool shadow and a few short curved opaque highlight swaths following the lock. Uneven occasionally broken patches, not shards, scratches or dense thin strands.
ANIME FACE: clear strong upper-lid shapes, simple iris color masses and two or three small highlights. Economical nose indicated by a restrained shadow, simple thin mouth mark, warm ivory skin and broad clean cheeks. Restrained cool-gray fringe and chin shadows. Face more resolved than clothes, not polished semi-realistic fantasy painting.
BRUSHWORK: opaque economical hand-painted color masses with a handful of broad directional curved or elongated strokes. Deliberate firm handmade edges, a few gentle skin transitions allowed. Broad swaths and grouped shadows, not triangular mosaics. Selected irregular stroke endings and overlaps, not all-over grain. Visible broad strokes at hair turns and major cloth bends surrounded by quiet areas.
CLOTH: three or four principal broad folds with elongated painterly strokes; light cloth has two or three readable broad shadow shapes, no splintered white shards. Hands and props readable and simply painted.
COLOR AND BACKGROUND: muted coherent color masses, clear cool-warm distinction and concentrated chromatic accents rather than uniform brown-gray wash. Background shapes painted with a few broad sparse strokes; preserve the requested setting and its light.
SMALL STRUCTURAL REFINEMENT: slightly firmer anatomically placed fringe/temple cast shadow and small nose-underside shadow; a few purposeful tapered colored line accents at lower eyelids, jaw-to-neck, collar and principal shoulder/chest fold transitions. A couple of broad midtone strokes connect shoulder/chest volume. Structural accents, not extra texture. Keep broad cheeks and expressive painterly coverage, not uniform flat fill.
AVOID: tiny angular hair facets, dense flyaways, strand-by-strand hair, sharp triangle mosaics, glossy 3D, semi-realistic face, realistic skin shading, iris microdetails, airbrushed commercial splash art, global grain filters, uniform vector fill, dense background brushwork, invented accessories, text, watermark and UI.
```
